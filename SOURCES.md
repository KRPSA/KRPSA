## Automatic language selection

- GeoJS's official [source README](https://github.com/jloh/geojs) documents full CORS support and its public production API. Its [country endpoint documentation](https://github.com/jloh/geojs-io/blob/master/content/docs/v1/endpoints/country.md) documents `https://get.geojs.io/v1/ip/country` without an IP argument as a plain-text response containing only the requesting visitor's two-letter country code. These official sources were retrieved with TLS verification intact.
- The site uses this country-only endpoint for first-visit language selection, with a browser-language fallback and a short timeout. Explicit KO/EN choices always take priority. The site does not request precise location or store IP addresses. The third-party API necessarily receives the connection's IP address to determine its country.
- The development environment's proxy currently rejects live requests to `get.geojs.io` with CONNECT 403. The documented API contract and simulated browser responses can be validated here; live service reachability remains unverified until the environment's saved network configuration is applied.

## 2026-10-15 IPSC in Motion reel

`reel.js` draws a 28-second explainer that plays as the first hero slide (and renders to video with `scripts/reel-render.mjs`). Everything is drawn in code: no third-party footage, photographs or logos other than the KRPSA crest. The CZ Shadow 2 is a vector illustration made for the reel (see below); CZ and Shadow 2 are trademarks of Česká zbrojovka and are named only to identify the pistol. The stage, shot times, splits and score are an illustrative demonstration, not a real match result. The soundtrack is synthesised by `scripts/reel-audio.mjs` from the reel's cue sheet (no samples).

The development environment's network policy blocked direct downloads from ipsc.org, czub.cz, cz-usa.com and Wikipedia on 2026-10-15, so each fact below was cross-checked through web-search excerpts of the documents listed. Re-check against the current IPSC Handgun Rules (January 2026 edition) before quoting numbers elsewhere.

- **DVC and history** — Diligentia, Vis, Celeritas (accuracy, power, speed); IPSC founded at the Columbia Conference, Columbia, Missouri, May 1976; 2026 is the 50th anniversary. https://www.ipsc.org/?p=1015 , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Practical_Shooting_Confederation
- **Range commands** — “Make Ready”, “Are You Ready?” (no reply means ready; otherwise “Not ready”), “Standby” with the start signal 1–4 seconds later (rule 8.3.3), “If You Are Finished, Unload and Show Clear”, “If Clear, Hammer Down, Holster”, “Range Is Clear”. IPSC Handgun Competition Rules, January 2024: https://www.ipsc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/IPSC-Handgun-Competition-Rules-Jan-2024-Edition-Final-27-Dec-2023.pdf
- **Scoring** — Comstock: hit factor = points ÷ time. Paper targets A 5 (Major and Minor), C 4 Major / 3 Minor, D 2 Major / 1 Minor; misses and no-shoot hits −10; scoring steel must fall to score and is worth 5. Same rule book; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Practical_Shooting_Confederation
- **Production division** — only handguns on the IPSC Production list; Minor only, power factor ≥ 125; at most 15 rounds in a magazine at the start signal; no optical sights; barrel ≤ 127 mm. https://www.ipsc.org/handgun-production-division/ , list: https://www.ipsc.org/rules/divlistP.php
- **Targets** — IPSC cardboard target 46 × 58 cm (A zone 15 cm wide, C zone 30 cm wide, D zone to the edge), e.g. https://doubletapsports.com/product/ipsc-classic-targets-50/ and https://www.drummondshooting.ca/product/ipsc-zone-target/ . Zone heights are drawn in proportion, not as rule dimensions. IPSC Popper 85 cm tall with a round head and narrow base: https://www.ipscgear.com/product-page/ipsc-full-size-steel-popper-with-holes . The 180° rule and freestyle course solving are general IPSC principles described in the same rule book.
- **CZ Shadow 2 drawing** — the outline, trigger guard, trigger, grip panel and base pad are traced from a left-side product photograph supplied by the site owner on 2026-10-15, mirrored to a right-side view and scaled to the pistol's ≈217 mm overall length; right-side details (ejection port, extractor, safety paddle, slide-stop pin) are placed from the same measurements. The reel names the pistol but no longer lists its specifications. The Production check shows the barrel (≈124 mm, 4.89 in) against the 127 mm limit and the Shadow 2's listing in Production: https://www.shootingillustrated.com/content/review-cz-shadow-2-pistol/ , https://www.ipsc.org/rules/divlistP.php
